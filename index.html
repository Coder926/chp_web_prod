Services

Everything Your Boiler Service Covers

CHP Home provides boiler servicing and gas safety checks for homeowners across Greater Manchester and the North West.

Gas tightness test

Checks for gas leaks throughout the system — a legal safety requirement for all gas appliances.

Internal part check & clean-up

Inspection and cleaning of key internal components to maintain efficiency and extend boiler life.

Flue terminal inspection

Ensures the flue is correctly fitted, unobstructed, and safely expelling combustion gases.

Boiler operations & controls check-up

Tests thermostat, programmer, and all controls to confirm everything is responding correctly.

Radiator heat check

Confirms all radiators are heating evenly and identifies any cold spots or system pressure issues.

Boiler Service FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking your boiler service or gas safety check.

Our boiler service includes a gas tightness test, internal part check & clean-up, flue terminal inspection, boiler operations & controls check-up, and radiator heat check — plus a signed boiler service record if your boiler passes, or a gas safety warning notice if it doesn't. Optional add-ons available: Unvented Cylinder Service and Central Heating Inhibitor Refill. See the Services section for current pricing.
From £79 inc VAT for the boiler service — the final price depends on which optional add-ons you choose (see the Services section above for full pricing). The price shown is the price you pay, confirmed before you check out — no call-out fees, no hidden extras. You pay securely online when booking.
Yes — every CHP Home engineer holds a current Gas Safe registration. It is a legal requirement to work on gas appliances in the UK. You can verify any engineer's credentials at GasSafeRegister.co.uk using their licence number, which our engineer will provide on request.
We currently look after homes across Manchester, Bolton, Wigan, Stockport, Warrington, Liverpool, Crewe and Stoke-on-Trent. Enter your postcode when you book and we'll confirm you're covered. Start your booking, or get in touch if you're unsure.
Yes — you can book your boiler service entirely online in under 2 minutes, with no phone call and no home survey needed. Enter your postcode to check availability, pick a date and time, and pay securely by card.
Yes, in full. You have a statutory 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, giving you an automatic right to cancel and a full refund within 14 days of booking. Beyond that, there's still no cancellation deadline — however close to your appointment you cancel, we'll refund the full amount to the card used for payment. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.
Only if your home has an unvented hot water cylinder, usually found in an airing cupboard or utility room — if you have a combi boiler with no cylinder, you won't need this add-on. We recommend having it serviced annually to check the safety devices and valves are working correctly; this can also help meet your manufacturer's warranty requirements, so check your cylinder's warranty terms for specifics.
In short: any extra work is always quoted separately and entirely optional — never carried out without your agreement. If your boiler passes, you'll receive a signed boiler service record. If it doesn't pass, we'll issue a gas safety warning notice.
